A taste of the Masia

More Than a Wine Tasting

Step inside the old cellar of Masia St Salvador 1582 and discover a relaxed, personal way to enjoy Catalan wine and cava. We'll share a selection of local flavours, stories of the region and one of our favourite traditions: drinking from a porró.

It's a lovely way to begin a family holiday, celebrate a birthday or spend an evening together — right here at the Masia, without having to travel anywhere.

The experience

Sip, Share & Discover

A private moment for your group, hosted in the intimate atmosphere of our cellar.

01

Wine & Cava

Discover a selection of Catalan wines and sparkling cava, with an informal introduction to local winemaking traditions.

02

The Porró Tradition

Learn how to drink from a traditional Catalan porró — a playful local custom that always brings smiles.

03

Stories in the Cellar

Enjoy the historic setting, hear a little about the Masia and share a memorable moment with family or friends.

For guests of Masia 1582

Make Your Stay Memorable

Ideal for birthdays, family gatherings and relaxed evenings together. The cellar is cosy and best suited to groups of approximately 12–14 people.
