Location

How to Get Here

Masia St Salvador 1582 is located in Cubelles, a quiet town between Sitges and Vilanova i la Geltrú on the Catalan coast.

Distances

  • Cubelles Beaches 10 min drive
  • Vilanova i la Geltrú 10 min drive
  • Sitges 15 min drive
  • Barcelona Airport (BCN) 30 min drive
  • Barcelona City Centre 45 min drive
  • PortAventura World 40 min drive

Getting Here

From Barcelona Airport

Take the C-32 motorway south towards Sitges/Tarragona. Exit at Cubelles (exit 52). The masia is approximately 5 minutes from the motorway exit. We will send detailed last-mile directions upon booking confirmation.

By Taxi / Transfer

A taxi from Barcelona Airport costs approximately €60–80. We can recommend reliable transfer services if needed.
