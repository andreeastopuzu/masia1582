Historic Architecture Meets Modern Comfort

Built in 1582, this traditional Catalan masia has been lovingly restored to preserve its authentic character while adding all the comforts of contemporary living. Original stone walls, hand-laid Catalan floor tiles, and wooden beams remain, creating an authentic Mediterranean atmosphere.

The restoration was completed in 2022 with meticulous attention to detail. Every decision honored the building's 440-year heritage while introducing modern heating, air conditioning, high-speed WiFi, and a fully equipped kitchen.
