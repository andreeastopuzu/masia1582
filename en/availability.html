Booking Terms

Minimum Stay

Minimum stay varies by season: 2 nights in low season, 3 nights in shoulder season, 5 nights during peak summer (June-August).

Check-in / Check-out

Standard check-in is 5:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out may be possible with advance notice for €50 each.

Cancellation Policy

Flexible cancellation up to 30 days before arrival. For cancellations within 30 days, 50% of the booking amount is non-refundable. No refunds for cancellations within 7 days.

Booking Process

To secure your dates, a 30% deposit is required upon booking confirmation. Full payment is due 14 days before your arrival date.
