Private Cava Wine Tour

Explore the Penedès wine region and taste authentic Spanish sparkling wine from your private estate

Wine Experience

The Heart of Cava Country

Masia St Salvador 1582 is located in the heart of the Penedès wine region, where over 80% of the world's Cava sparkling wine is produced. We arrange exclusive, private wine tours that showcase the best local wineries, vineyards, and producers.

Your Experience

What's Included in the Cava Tour

Winery Visit

Private guided tour of a traditional Cava winery where you'll learn about the méthode champenoise production process and the region's winemaking heritage.

Vineyard Walk

Stroll through working vineyards and understand how the unique microclimate and soil composition create exceptional Cava.

Cava Tasting

Sample a selection of premium Cava varieties from brut to sweet, paired with local Catalan charcuterie and cheeses.

Local Guide

Expert sommelier or winemaker guide who shares stories about the region's wine culture, traditions, and family producers.

Tour Options

Half-Day Tour (4 hours)

Perfect introduction to the region. Includes one winery visit, vineyard walk, Cava tasting, and light refreshments.
