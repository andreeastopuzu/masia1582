The Property

Masia St Salvador 1582

A restored 16th-century Catalan country house surrounded by vineyards, olive trees, Mediterranean forest, and sea views.

Set on a private 60,000 m² estate in Cubelles, just 15 minutes from Sitges and 30 minutes from Barcelona Airport, the masia offers the perfect blend of heritage, nature, and modern comfort for up to 12 guests.

Restored in 2022 with attention to preserving heritage and maintaining its original character — from the original stone walls to the traditional Catalan tiles — while incorporating modern amenities for a comfortable stay.

The Space

Where Heritage Meets Modern Comfort

The property features 5 spacious bedrooms, 4 modern bathrooms, generous living areas, and a fully equipped kitchen. The interior preserves original stone walls and traditional Catalan tiles, creating an atmosphere that connects you to over 440 years of history.

Outside, guests have exclusive access to the entire 60,000 m² estate: the private garden, swimming pool with sun terrace, outdoor dining areas, vineyards, olive groves, and Mediterranean forest with walking paths.

The masia is rented as an entire property, ensuring complete privacy for your group. In some periods, a separate unit may be available for smaller groups.
