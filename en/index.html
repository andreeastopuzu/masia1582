Welcome

A Place Where Time Slows Down

Masia St Salvador 1582 is a restored 16th-century Catalan country house set on a private 6-hectare estate in Cubelles, overlooking the Mediterranean Sea. Surrounded by vineyards, olive trees, and forests, the estate offers space, privacy, and an authentic Mediterranean experience for families and groups.

Restored in 2022 with deep respect for its original stone walls and traditional Catalan tiles, the masia blends 440 years of heritage with modern comfort — pool, air conditioning, full kitchen, and high-speed WiFi.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is the masia from Barcelona airport?

Masia St Salvador 1582 is just 30 minutes by car from Barcelona-El Prat Airport (BCN). We can provide detailed driving directions or recommend taxi/transfer services.

What is the minimum stay?

Minimum stay varies by season: 2 nights in low season, up to 5 nights during peak summer months. Contact us for your specific dates.

Are pets allowed at the property?

Yes! Pets are always welcome at Masia St Salvador 1582. We ask only that pets are not allowed in the swimming pool. The 6-hectare estate provides plenty of space for dogs to explore.

Is the pool private?

Absolutely. The swimming pool is exclusively for your use during your stay. It is surrounded by a private garden and sun terrace with views of the Mediterranean Sea.

Can we host events or celebrations?

Yes, celebrations and events are possible with prior approval from the owners. Please mention your event plans when making your booking inquiry so we can discuss details.

What is the check-in and check-out time?

Check-in is from 5:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late check-out may be possible depending on availability — just ask.
