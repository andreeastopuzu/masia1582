Our Story

A passion project restoring 440 years of Catalan heritage

Our Mission

Preserving Catalan Heritage

Masia St Salvador 1582 represents more than a luxury villa rental. It's a commitment to preserving authentic Catalan architecture and hospitality traditions while providing guests with unforgettable Mediterranean experiences. We believe that travel should connect people with real places, real history, and real communities.

History

From 1582 to Today

1582

The Beginning

Original Construction

Masia St Salvador was built during the Spanish Golden Age, a traditional Catalan country house for agricultural and wine production. For over four centuries, it remained a working farm and vineyard, home to generations of Catalan families.

1900s-2020

Changing Times

Evolution

As Catalonia modernized, the masia evolved with the times. The vineyards remained productive, but the structure required preservation work. The property was lovingly maintained but needed restoration to ensure its survival for future generations.

2022

Restoration

A Careful Restoration

We undertook a meticulous restoration project, guided by a philosophy of respect for history. Original stone walls were preserved, Catalan floor tiles were carefully restored, and wooden beams were treated with care. Modern comforts—WiFi, heating, air conditioning, full kitchen—were thoughtfully integrated without compromising the building's authentic character.

What We Believe

Our Values

Authenticity

We preserve the real character of this historic property. No kitsch, no shortcuts — just genuine Catalan charm combined with modern comfort.

Sustainability

Our restoration honored the property's heritage while ensuring sustainability. We maintain the vineyards, respect the land, and operate with environmental consciousness.

Hospitality

We offer warm, personal hospitality grounded in Catalan traditions. Direct communication, local recommendations, and genuine care for our guests' experience.

Local Commitment

Part of the Community

We're committed to supporting the local Cubelles and Penedès communities. We work with local guides for wine tours, recommend local restaurants and artisans, and contribute to preserving the cultural heritage that makes this region special. When you stay at Masia St Salvador, you're supporting Catalan businesses and communities.
