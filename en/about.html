Our Story

Welcome to Masia St Salvador 1582

Dated from 1582, this 60,000-square-metre estate overlooking the Mediterranean Sea was restored in 2022 with attention to preserving heritage and maintaining its original character.

After years of living a fast-paced urban life, we longed for a simpler way of living, closer to nature and rooted in local traditions. We traveled the world in search of inspiration, but always came back to our heritage, which we felt was the perfect place to bring our “simple living” vision to life.

With a deep respect for the history and architecture of the house, we carefully restored every detail — from the original stone walls to the traditional Catalan tiles — while incorporating modern amenities to ensure guests have a comfortable stay.

The swimming pool, vineyards, lush gardens, olive groves, and lemon trees surrounding the property are carefully maintained to provide a peaceful retreat from the hustle and bustle of modern life.

Philosophy

Slow Living in Catalonia

Heritage Preservation

Every renovation decision honoured the building’s 440+ year history. We believe these old walls have stories to tell, and our role is to preserve them for future generations.

Sustainable Tourism

We promote responsible tourism that respects the environment and the local community. Our goal is to leave this land better than we found it.

Local Community

We work closely with local producers and businesses to provide an authentic Catalan experience, supporting the community that makes this region special.

Authentic Experience

From the wine on your table to the olive oil in your kitchen, we connect guests with the traditions and flavours of the Penedès region.

“We invite you to come and experience the tranquility for yourself, and we hope that your stay with us will be a memorable one.”
