Private Cava & Wine Tour

Discover the Penedès wine region — where over 80% of the world’s cava is produced — on a private guided tour from the masia.

An Unforgettable Wine Experience

Masia St Salvador 1582 is located within the renowned D.O. Penedès wine region, celebrated for its centuries-old winemaking tradition and world-famous cava sparkling wine.

Our private tour takes you to two carefully selected wineries where you'll discover the art of cava production, taste premium wines, and enjoy the stunning vineyard landscapes of the Catalan countryside.

Request Tour Information

Tour Details

6–7 Hours

A full-day experience starting at 9:00 AM with comfortable pacing between wineries and scenic viewpoints.

Private & Exclusive

Your group only. No strangers, no schedules to follow. A personalised experience tailored to your preferences.

2 Wineries

Visit two distinct bodegas in the D.O. Penedès region plus scenic viewpoints of the vineyard landscape.

What’s Included

  • Pickup from Masia St Salvador 1582
  • Air-conditioned van transport
  • Visits to two D.O. Penedès wineries
  • 6+ premium wine and cava tastings
  • Olive oil tasting
  • Snacks and water

Good to Know

  • Start time: 9:00 AM
  • Available to guests of the masia
  • Meals and drinks beyond tastings not included
  • Contact the host before or upon arrival for pricing and scheduling
  • Scenic viewpoints included on the route
