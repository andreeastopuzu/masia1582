How to Get Here

By Car

From Barcelona Airport (30 minutes):

  1. Exit the airport and head towards Castelldefels
  2. Take the C-31 highway towards Sitges
  3. Follow signs for Cubelles
  4. Turn right on Ctra. el Corral d'en Tort
  5. Masia St Salvador will be on your left

Parking: Free private parking on the property for all guests.

By Train & Taxi

From Barcelona:

  1. Take the Renfe R2 train from Barcelona towards Sitges
  2. Get off at Cubelles station
  3. Arrange a taxi or car service to the masia (5 minutes)

Car Services: We can recommend reliable car services and arrange pickup if needed.
